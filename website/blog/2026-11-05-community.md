JaKtA's community lives on GitHub: ask questions, propose ideas and show what you built with JaKtA in our
[Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/jakta-bdi/discussions).

<!-- truncate -->

## Discussions

- **[Q&A](https://github.com/orgs/jakta-bdi/discussions/categories/q-a)**: stuck on something? Ask here, the
  maintainers and other users will help.
- **[Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/jakta-bdi/discussions/categories/ideas)**: a feature you would like in JaKtA.
- **[Show and tell](https://github.com/orgs/jakta-bdi/discussions/categories/show-and-tell)**: an agent system,
  a simulation or a project built with JaKtA.
- **[Announcements](https://github.com/orgs/jakta-bdi/discussions/categories/announcements)**: updates from the
  maintainers.

## Issues and contributions

Found a bug, or something in the documentation that is wrong or unclear? Open an
[issue](https://github.com/jakta-bdi/jakta/issues). Pull requests are welcome too: the code, the examples and this
website all live in the [jakta repository](https://github.com/jakta-bdi/jakta).
